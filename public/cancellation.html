Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Cancellation by Customer

We understand plans change. For confirmed bookings:

  • 7+ days before departure: 100% full refund or free trip date rescheduling.
  • 3 to 6 days before departure: 70% refund (30% retained for homestay & guide reservations).
  • Less than 72 hours before departure: 50% refund.

2. Cancellation due to Weather / Road Blockades

If a trip is cancelled by the operator due to severe mountain weather, severe road landslides, or official district travel advisories, travelers receive a 100% full refund or priority rescheduling.

3. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source within 3 to 5 business days.
