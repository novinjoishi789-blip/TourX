Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. Booking Request Workflow

Submitting a booking form on Explore Dima Hasao Tourism constitutes a Booking Request (PENDING). A booking is formally confirmed only after our team verifies local guide and accommodation availability and issues an explicit confirmation notification.

2. Travel Requirements & Permits

Travelers must carry valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID). Forest permits for sanctuary areas such as Borail Range are processed by our guides in accordance with Assam Forest Department regulations.

3. Mountain Weather & Itinerary Flexibility

Hill terrain conditions in Dima Hasao can be affected by monsoon rains or landslides. In the interest of traveler safety, local guides reserve the right to alter mountain routes or trek schedules when weather conditions require.

4. Traveler Responsibility

Travelers are expected to follow local Dimasa village etiquette, respect sacred sites, adhere to Leave No Trace environmental standards, and follow guide instructions during wilderness treks.
