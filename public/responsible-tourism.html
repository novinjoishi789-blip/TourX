Responsible & Eco-Tourism Code

Our Commitment to Dima Hasao's Hills, Wildlife, & Communities

🌿 1. Support Local Indigenous Economy

100% of our guiding fees, homestay payouts, and trail meal sourcing stay directly within Dimasa and hill tribe village communities in Haflong, Jatinga, Maibang, and Kopili valley.

♻️ 2. Zero Littering & Waste Management

We enforce a strict pack-it-in, pack-it-out plastic policy across all Borail Wildlife Sanctuary treks and Tumjang Peak campsites. Single-use plastic bottles are discouraged; reusable stainless-steel flasks are recommended.

🦅 3. Bird & Wildlife Conservation

During Jatinga's migratory bird season (late monsoon), flash photography, high-wattage searchlights, and loud sound systems are strictly prohibited in bird watch zones to protect avian species.

🏛️ 4. Cultural Respect

When visiting Maibang monolith ruins and sacred village sites, travelers are asked to follow local customs, remove shoes where requested, and ask permission before photographing village elders or handicraft artisans.
