Winter (October–March) offers the clearest skies and pleasant trekking weather. Monsoon (June–September) is best for Panimur Waterfall at full roaring capacity and Jatinga mist walks.

No special permits are required for Indian citizens to visit Dima Hasao or Haflong. Forest sanctuary permits for Borail are obtained by our local guides.

Haflong is well connected by direct trains from Guwahati (e.g. Vistadome / Jan Shatabdi) to New Haflong Station (NCB). You can also travel via road from Guwahati (approx. 8-9 hours) or Silchar (approx. 3.5 hours).
