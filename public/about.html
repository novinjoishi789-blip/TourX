Our Story

About Us

A small, local team trying to show Assam's hill country to more people — without wrecking what makes it special.

Why We Started

Built by people who grew up in these hills

Explore Dima Hasao began with a simple frustration: almost nobody outside Assam had heard of this district, despite it holding some of Northeast India's most striking landscapes. What started as informal weekend guiding for friends grew into a small team of local guides, homestay partners, and drivers — all from in and around Haflong — determined to show visitors this hill country the way we actually experience it.

Today we run small-group treks, sunrise camps, and heritage walks across all six signature destinations, partnering with village homestays instead of big hotel chains wherever we can.
