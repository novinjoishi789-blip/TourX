Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a trip request or contact inquiry on Explore Dima Hasao Tourism, we collect personal details including your name, email address, phone number, travel dates, and special requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are strictly used to coordinate tour itineraries, arrange local homestay accommodations, secure forest permits, and communicate trip updates with you via Email and WhatsApp.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing agencies. Information is shared only with assigned local Dimasa guides and licensed homestay hosts for your journey.

4. Cookies & Security

We use secure HttpOnly session cookies for customer Google Sign-In authentication. No private authentication credentials or secret keys are exposed to client script environments.

If you have any questions regarding data privacy, contact us at novinjoishi789@gmail.com.
